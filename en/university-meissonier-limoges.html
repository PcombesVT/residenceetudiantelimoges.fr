Student residence — Limoges La Borie

University Meissonier
Student residence in Limoges

The largest student residence managed by Begip in Limoges. 204 studios across 7 buildings, one-bedroom (T1bis) flats and a three-room (T3) flat, in the residential La Borie district. A 15-min walk from the Faculty of Sciences and the IUT. Eligible for APL/ALS.
