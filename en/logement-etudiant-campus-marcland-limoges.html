The health campus

Marcland, the health hub
of Limoges

To the south of Limoges, the Marcland campus brings together the University of Limoges' health schools: the Medical School, the School of Pharmacy and the School of Dentistry, right next to the CHU Dupuytren teaching hospital. The area also hosts, nearby, the IFSI (nursing) and the ILFOMER (physiotherapy, occupational therapy, speech therapy).

It is the campus of long days and frequent placements: living close to the CHU and the lecture halls changes everything. The E-Magine residence, in Isle, is the closest — but it is regularly full. Begip then directs you to its two La Borie residences (University Meissonier and University Dutreix), which reach the CHU by bus or bike, with studios available right away.

Frequently asked

Living at the health campus:
your questions

Which residence near the Medical School in Limoges?
E-Magine (Isle) is closest to the Marcland campus, ~12-14 min walk — but often full (waiting list via Begip). As an available alternative, the La Borie residences (Meissonier, Dutreix) reach the CHU by bus or bike, studios from €350/mo incl.
Where to live for pharmacy or dentistry?
Pharmacy and Dentistry are on the Marcland campus, next to Medicine and the CHU. Same logic: E-Magine closest, La Borie the available alternative 15-20 minutes away by transport.
Is the E-Magine residence full?
E-Magine is regularly full. Begip keeps a waiting list — contact the agency to join. In the meantime, Meissonier and Dutreix offer available studios, with access to the CHU by bus or bike.
What housing for IFSI or ILFOMER (rehabilitation)?
The IFSI and ILFOMER are near the Vanteaux area and the CHU. Depending on your classes and placements, E-Magine (CHU side) or the La Borie residences may suit — Begip helps you choose.
Housing near the CHU and the health schools?

Contact the Begip agency: E-Magine waiting list, or available studios in our La Borie residences with access to the CHU. Fees €130 all-in.
