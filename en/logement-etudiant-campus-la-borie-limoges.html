The La Borie campus

The science and engineering
hub of Limoges

To the north of Limoges, the La Borie area brings together much of the University of Limoges' science and engineering teaching: the Faculty of Science and Technology, the IUT du Limousin, the Faculty of Sport Science (STAPS), along with several sixth-form colleges offering BTS courses (Suzanne Valadon, Turgot). The ENSIL-ENSCI engineering school completes this technical cluster to the north of the city.

It is one of the most convenient districts for a student in science, technology or sport science: you live right next to the lecture halls while staying about ten minutes by bus from the city centre. It is also where Begip manages two of its three student residences — so you can live a stone's throw from your classes, with a genuine local contact.

Frequently asked

Living at the La Borie campus:
your questions

Which residence should I pick near the Science Faculty in Limoges?
Two Begip residences serve the La Borie campus, about a 15-minute walk from the Faculty of Science and Technology: University Meissonier (204 studios from €350/mo incl.) and University Dutreix (45 studios and one two-room flat from €378/mo). Both are eligible for APL/ALS.
What is the closest housing to the IUT du Limousin?
University Dutreix, at 231 rue Armand Dutreix, is about a 5-minute walk from the IUT. University Meissonier, in the La Borie district, is about a 15-minute walk. Both residences are managed by the Begip agency.
How much does student housing cost at the La Borie campus?
Studios start at €350/month including bills (Meissonier) and €378/month (Dutreix). With APL or ALS, your net cost can drop below €250/month depending on your income.
Where to live when studying at STAPS or ENSIL?
The science and engineering cluster of Limoges (Science Faculty, IUT, STAPS, and ENSIL-ENSCI to the north) is served from the La Borie campus. Both Meissonier and Dutreix are located there, with direct bus access to the rest of the city.
Looking for housing at the La Borie campus?

Contact the Begip agency for current availability in our two La Borie campus residences, to arrange a viewing and prepare your application. Fees €130 all-in.
