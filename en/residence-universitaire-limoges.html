Understanding the options

CROUS or private residence
in Limoges: what’s the difference?

In Limoges, two types of university residence coexist: CROUS residences and private residences. Here’s what sets them apart to help you choose.

Public residences
CROUS de Limoges
Public student-welfare management
Access Priority for grant-holders on means-tested criteria. Application via messervices.etudiant.gouv.fr
Rates Subsidised for grant-holders — often between €200 and €350/month
Allocation By waiting list and means-tested criteria. Times vary by application
Facilities Vary by residence. Furniture often provided
APL Eligible depending on the residence
Private residences
Begip residences
University Meissonier · Dutreix · Vanteaux · E-Magine
Access Open to all students — no need to be a grant-holder. Standard application
Rates From €350/month incl. charges — net cost from ~€175/month with APL/ALS
Allocation First come, first served. Quick response, real-time availability
Facilities WiFi included, free gym, launderette, bike storage, CCTV
APL ✓ Eligible for APL or ALS — up to €180/month of benefit

💡 Begip’s tip: if you’re not a grant-holder or you want a home available immediately, Begip’s private residences offer an excellent alternative with good value for money, especially once APL or ALS is deducted.
