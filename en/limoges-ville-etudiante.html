Why Limoges

Studying in Limoges:
a city made for students

Often underrated next to the big cities, Limoges is one of the best cities in France to study with peace of mind. The capital of the Haute-Vienne, it is home to about 17,500 students around the University of Limoges — a multidisciplinary university — and a network of well-regarded schools: engineering (ENSIL-ENSCI), art (ENSA Limoges), management (IAE), health (IFSI), not forgetting BTS courses and preparatory classes.

Its greatest asset comes down to two words: human scale. Here you don’t lose an hour on transport to reach your campus, you find housing easily, and a student’s monthly budget stretches much further than elsewhere. That’s exactly what this guide sets out: how much student life costs in Limoges, where the campuses are, which districts to live in, which benefits to claim and how to find your home.

The real advantage

The cost of student living
in Limoges: among the lowest in France

In the annual student cost-of-living rankings, Limoges regularly features among the cheapest cities in France. And it shows across every spending category — not just rent.

🏠
Housing
The biggest item in the budget, and this is where Limoges makes the difference. A studio rents from ~€280/month (self-contained studio) and from €350 including charges in a serviced residence — far below big-city prices. Eligible for APL/ALS.
🚌
Getting around
A compact city: many students live without a car. The STCL bus network covers the whole conurbation with attractive youth passes, and most journeys are made on foot or by bike.
🍽️
Eating
The Crous university canteens offer a full meal at a subsidised price (~€3.30, €1 for grant-holders). Markets, local shops and moderate local prices lighten the grocery budget further.
🎭
Going out & leisure
A lively but affordable student life: cafés, concerts, cinema, clubs and campus events at reasonable prices. You enjoy the city without blowing your budget.

💡 The takeaway: on the same budget, a student lives more comfortably in Limoges than in most large university cities. As rent is the heaviest item, it’s also the first place to save — which is why it pays to choose your home well from the start.

Where to live

Housing in Limoges:
the 3 main options

Depending on your budget, independence and campus, three options are open to you. All are eligible for CAF benefit.

The most services
Student residence
A studio in a residence dedicated to students, with services included (WiFi, launderette, security, sometimes a gym). A student atmosphere and peace of mind for parents. From €350/month incl. charges.

The most affordable
Self-contained studio
A furnished or unfurnished studio in the city’s general rental market, in the district of your choice. The most flexible option and often the cheapest, from ~€280/month. Ideal if you want to optimise your budget.

Flatshare
Sharing a larger flat between several people: more space and comfort for a controlled rent per person. A good option for tight budgets that want space and group living.

The student districts of Limoges

La Borie appeals to students in sciences, at the IUT or in STAPS: a leafy setting with shops and the science campus close by. The city centre suits those studying law, arts or at a business school who want everything within reach (faculties, station, nightlife). The Vanteaux district, historically student, remains very sought after. Finally, around Isle and the health area, there’s a quiet, residential setting popular with students in medicine and nursing.

Housing benefit

Cutting your rent further:
the benefits to know

Once you’ve found your home, several schemes bring the bill down. In Limoges, with rents already low, the effect is even more striking.

💶
APL / ALS (CAF)
CAF housing benefit can cut your rent by €100 to €180/month depending on your situation. Begip homes are eligible. Estimate it at caf.fr.
🎓
Crous grants
The means-tested grant (student social application) can be combined with APL or ALS. Two forms of support that add up to a minimal net cost.
🤝
Application support
A local agency for over 30 years, Begip helps you put together a strong application and find the guarantee solution suited to your situation. A real person, not a call centre.
