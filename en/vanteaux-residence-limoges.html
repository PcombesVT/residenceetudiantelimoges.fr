Your questions about student
housing in Vanteaux

Everything you need to know about student housing in the Vanteaux district of Limoges.

Yes, the Vanteaux district is historically the health-students’ district in Limoges. It’s close to the faculties of medicine, pharmacy and dentistry and to the hospital. It’s one of the most sought-after districts for students on medical and paramedical courses.
Contact the Begip agency on 05 55 33 97 50 or by email at agence@begip.fr. The team will tell you current availability and arrange a viewing by appointment. It’s best to contact the agency in the spring for the September start, as studios in the Vanteaux district are in high demand.
Yes, the homes managed by Begip in the Vanteaux district are eligible for CAF benefit. Depending on the legal status of the home, you’ll receive APL (approved homes) or ALS (non-approved). In both cases, the benefit can cut your rent by €100 to €180/month. Run an estimate at caf.fr.
Yes, the Vanteaux district is a quiet residential and student district in Limoges. Historically home to students and families, it has a pleasant atmosphere, local shops and good public transport. Begip residences have badge access control.
Yes, the Vanteaux district has local shops within walking distance: bakeries, mini-markets, pharmacies, restaurants. Neighbourhood life is active thanks to the student density. Limoges city centre is about ten minutes away by STCL bus for the big-name stores and cultural venues.
