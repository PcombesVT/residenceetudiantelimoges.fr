Student housing in Limoges

Why choose
a student residence in Limoges?

A student residence is the all-in-one solution for your housing in Limoges: an unfurnished flat or one with furniture provided, on-site services, security and proximity to the university campuses.

🏠

Unfurnished or with furniture provided

At University Meissonier, all homes come with furniture provided. At University Dutreix, you choose: unfurnished or furnished, subject to availability. In every case, the kitchenette is equipped and fitted cupboards are included.

💶

Clear student rent and charges

The rent covers all or part of the charges depending on the residence. The electricity contract is your responsibility. Internet is included at University Meissonier and E-Magine.

🏦

CAF benefit: APL or ALS

Depending on the status of each residence, your home is eligible for the CAF’s APL or ALS — to significantly reduce what you actually pay each month.

🔐

Safety and peace of mind

Access control, door entry phones and CCTV depending on the residence. You and your parents can rest easy in a secure environment.

🏫

Close to the campuses and universities of Limoges

Our student residences are a few minutes on foot or by transport from the main faculties, IUT and grandes écoles of Limoges.

🤝

Student community life

Common areas, a gym, a study room, green spaces: the residence encourages students in Limoges to meet and help one another.

📋

Personalised Begip support

Reduced fees from €130 all-in on directly managed residences, responsive local management, a dedicated contact at Begip Limoges.

🅿️

Private parking at every residence

Every residence has secure private parking. Included in the rent at University Dutreix and E-Magine, available at extra cost at University Meissonier.
